# Squeeze the Beat

A Frankenstein prototype of a squeeze-ball alarm for blind and low-vision sleepers.

**Try the app:** https://ycjanetchen.github.io/Squeeze_the_Beat/

## The idea

A squeeze ball with buttons and a speaker added. The phone app connects to the ball over Bluetooth to set the alarm and choose the music. When the alarm rings, squeeze the ball on every beat to turn it off.

## What's in the demo

- **Three ways to set the alarm:** say it, swipe the wheels (works with VoiceOver), or set it on the ball itself.
- **Squeeze on the beat:** one loud drum hit about once a second. Only on-beat squeezes count (choose 4, 8 or 12). Hold the squeeze for 2 seconds to snooze.
- **Spoken feedback:** the ball reads every new alarm time and its battery level out loud.
- **Magnetic holder:** keeps the ball from rolling, holds it in the same position every time, and charges it.
- **Ball styles:** basketball, baseball or football (soccer).

## How to use it

1. Open the link on a phone or computer and tap once to turn on sound.
2. Tap or click the ball (or press Space) to squeeze it.
3. Press **Ring now** to test the alarm, then squeeze on every beat to stop it.

On iPhone, turn up the media volume if you hear nothing.
